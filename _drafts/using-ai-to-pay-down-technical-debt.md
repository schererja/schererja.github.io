One of the more useful things a team can do with AI is finally get to the work that’s been on the back burner. There’s usually something in the backlog everyone knows about and nobody has time to touch. Maybe it’s an old integration that’s hard to troubleshoot or a maintenance task that still depends on someone remembering all the steps.

## Put some of the time back into maintenance

Technical debt can stay quiet for a long time. Then something breaks, the team has to fix it in a hurry, and suddenly there’s no time to understand why it got there in the first place. If an AI tool saves time, I’d like to see some of that time used before the problem becomes urgent. Use it to get oriented in unfamiliar code, trace a dependency, or identify what needs a closer look. Give an engineer time to follow through and verify the change.

## Keep the people who know the system

People who’ve worked on a system for years know why some of its decisions were made and what to watch for when it changes. It’s hard to assume that knowledge will still be there if those people are let go. Even if a company later decides it needs them back, they may have moved on or may have a good reason to decline.

When I think about the value of AI at work, I keep coming back to a simpler question. What could the team finally fix if it had time to do the job properly?

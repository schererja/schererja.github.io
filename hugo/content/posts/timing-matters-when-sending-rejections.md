Rejection emails are part of applying for work, and I understand why companies automate them. I’ve been thinking about one detail those systems could handle better: when the email goes out.

Getting a rejection over Thanksgiving or Christmas can interrupt the time someone has set aside with family, especially when they’re already under pressure from a long search. If a company already schedules its notifications, it could set aside holidays and deliver routine decisions during the next working day.

I still want employers to communicate their decisions promptly. For messages that can wait a day, though, choosing when to send them is a simple way to be considerate.

A video about employees waiting for layoff news at Amazon got me thinking about what happens before and after an announcement. It’s hard to focus on your work when you don’t know if your job, your team, or the project you’re on will still be there the next day.

The uncertainty can last well after the decisions are announced. People may have lost colleagues they relied on. Priorities and responsibilities can change at the same time, and it takes a while for a team to figure out how to work together again.

If a company is preparing for layoffs, I think it should also plan for that period afterward. Tell people what you know, be clear about what they’re expected to take on, and give them time to reset priorities. The work that remains still needs a team capable of doing it.

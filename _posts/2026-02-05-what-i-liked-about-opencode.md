I’ve tried a few different tools for planning and developing projects with AI. OpenCode stood out because its interface was quick to use and I could work with different models in the same place. I could also try options that were free to use during my evaluation, which made it easier to experiment.

## The interface matters during a working session

I spend a lot of time going back and forth between a question, an answer, and the next detail I need to check. The less friction there is in that loop, the easier it is for me to stay with the problem. I liked that OpenCode gave me room to choose a model or variant and adjust the interface to my taste.

I also tried Claude Code and saw reasons to use it. OpenCode was the tool I wanted to keep exploring at that point because of how the experience fit my workflow. I hadn’t spent much time with MCP servers or skills yet, so this was an early impression based on the parts I’d actually used.

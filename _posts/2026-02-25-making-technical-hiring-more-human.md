A long job search can wear people down. Applications, interviews, and uncertainty take up time and energy, especially when someone has recently lost a job. If you know someone in that position, it’s worth checking in on how they’re doing.

## Give candidates room to have a conversation

By the time a candidate interviews with you, they may have gone through a lot of interviews elsewhere. If someone seems tired or struggles to come up with questions, there can be more going on than you can see in that conversation. Giving them a little time to think and a clear sense of what to expect can make it easier for both sides to figure out whether the role fits.

## Send decisions during working hours

A rejection is never great news, but the time it arrives can make a difference. When possible, I’d like to see companies send those decisions during regular working hours. It’s a small change that can spare someone the surprise of seeing a rejection early in the morning or over the weekend.

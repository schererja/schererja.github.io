One of the most frustrating parts of a job search is the silence. I understand that some openings get far more applicants than a hiring team can respond to individually. A short email telling someone the company has decided to move on still gives them closure and lets them concentrate on other opportunities.

## Follow up after interviews

Once someone has done a few rounds of interviews or an assessment, they’ve made a real investment in the process. If the team can share a specific thing the candidate could work on, that feedback can be valuable. If policy or time limits what the team can say, even a clear decision is worth sending.

Candidates learn about a company from the way it communicates. Closing the loop is a small part of the hiring process, and it can make a big difference to the person waiting on the other end.
